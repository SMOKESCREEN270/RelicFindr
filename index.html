What is this place?

Since the 1960s, NASA has sent robots and astronauts to other worlds. Almost none of that hardware came home. Landers stay where they land, and probes keep flying until the power runs out. This site is a museum for those machines: what they were built to do, what they discovered, and where they are now.

01

Why robots first?

Space is far away and dangerous. Robots scout the way, test the ground and show us what to expect before people go.

02

Why leave them?

Bringing hardware home costs far more than it is worth. When the power or fuel is gone, the machine stays.

03

Silent, not useless

Their data still fills textbooks, and a few devices, such as mirrors on the Moon, still work today.

Every mission leaves something behind.

Some relics are silent monuments. Others kept doing science for years, or still do. As exploration grows, the question is no longer only how we get there, but what we leave and what it costs. Explore it the way the museum curators do: through the picture.
